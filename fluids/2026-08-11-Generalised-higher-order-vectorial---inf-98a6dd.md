# Generalised higher order vectorial $\infty$-eigenvalue problems

- **作者 (Authors)**: William Chang, Nikos Katzourakis
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-11
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10531v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究了最小化一类映射上第 $k$ 导数的函数的 $L^\infty$ 范数的问题，受到涉及映射函数的 $L^\infty$ 范数及其低阶导数的约束，对于任何整数 $k\geq 2$。我们施加与经典“夹紧”和“铰接”情况的 $k$ 阶类似物相对应的边界条件。通过使用$L^p$近似方法，我们建立了一个特殊的$L^\infty$极小化器的存在性，它求解以测度系数为参数的散度偏微分方程组。该系统构成了所考虑的约束变分问题的阿伦森-欧拉方程的对应系统。此外，我们建立了特征值的下界。目前的工作将 Clark 和 Katzourakis 的二阶向量结果 [广义二阶向量 $\infty$-特征值问题，PRSE A, 1-21, 2024] 扩展到一般的高阶设置。

## 英文摘要

We study the problem of minimising the $L^\infty$ norm of a function of the $k$-th derivative over a class of maps, subject to a constraint involving the $L^\infty$ norm of a function of the map and its lower-order derivatives, for any integer $k\geq 2$. We impose boundary conditions corresponding to the $k$-th order analogues of the classical ``clamped'' and ``hinged'' cases. By employing the method of $L^p$ approximations, we establish the existence of a special $L^\infty$ minimiser, which solves a divergence PDE system with measure coefficients as parameters. This system constitutes the counterpart of the Aronsson--Euler equations for the constrained variational problem under consideration. Furthermore, we establish a lower bound for the eigenvalue. The present work extends the second-order vectorial results of Clark and Katzourakis [Generalised second order vectorial $\infty$-eigenvalue problems, PRSE A, 1-21, 2024] to the general higher-order setting.
