# Unified theory for regularity persistence of vortex patch boundaries

- **作者 (Authors)**: Marc Magaña
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-03
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02033v1)

## 中文摘要

我们建立了一个统一的局部理论，用于说明在具有径向卷积核 $K(|x-y|)$ 的二维主动标量方程族中涡斑边界 Sobolev 正则性的持久性。该类包括二维欧拉方程、局部可积范围 $0<β<1$ 内的广义 SQG 方程以及准地转浅水方程。在$K$的自然假设（平滑性、原点附近可积性、单调性和多项式增长）下，我们证明如果初始边界属于$H^3(\mathbb T)$并且满足弧弦条件，那么轮廓动力学方程在$C([0,T];H^3(\mathbb T))$中存在唯一的局部解。在核上更强的可积性条件下，我们还获得了$H^2$解的局部存在性。该证明将轮廓方程的 Sobolev 能量估计与弧弦数量的定量控制结合起来。

## 英文摘要

We establish a unified local theory for the persistence of Sobolev regularity of vortex patch boundaries in a family of two-dimensional active scalar equations with radial convolution kernels $K(|x-y|)$. The class includes the 2D Euler equation, the generalized SQG equation in the locally integrable range $0<β<1$, and the quasi-geostrophic shallow water equation. Under natural assumptions on $K$ (smoothness, integrability near the origin, monotonicity, and polynomial growth), we prove that if the initial boundary belongs to $H^3(\mathbb T)$ and satisfies the arc-chord condition, then the contour dynamics equation admits a unique local solution in $C([0,T];H^3(\mathbb T))$. Under a stronger integrability condition on the kernel, we also obtain local existence of $H^2$ solutions. The proof combines Sobolev energy estimates for the contour equation with quantitative control of the arc-chord quantity.
