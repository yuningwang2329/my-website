# Generalized solution and Weak-Strong uniqueness for a barotropic Euler-Riesz system

- **作者 (Authors)**: Nilasis Chaudhuri
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-03
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01872v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究环面 $\mathbb T^d$, $d=2,3$ 上的 Euler--Riesz 系统：正压压力 $p(\varrho)=a\varrho^γ$ ($γ>1$, $a>0$) 的可压缩欧拉方程，耦合到带有 Riesz 核的非局部排斥力 ($\approx \varrho \nabla_x K \ast \varrho$) $K(x)\propto|x|^{β-d}$ 阶 $β\in(0,2)$。由于 $K$ 是反分数拉普拉斯 $(-Δ)^{-β/2}$ 的核，因此我们通过 Caffarelli--Silvestre 扩展将力重新转换为局部应力张量的迹，用一个额外变量中的局部恒等式替换非局部相互作用。对于排斥核，总能量是强制的，我们用它来引入任意大的有限能量数据的全局时间 \emph{耗散解} 的概念。我们的主要结果是弱（测量值）——强唯一性，对于每个阶 $β\in(0,2)$ 和每个 $γ>1$ 都是独立的：在存在强解的任何区间上，具有相同初始数据的每个耗散解都与其重合，并且所有缺陷都消失。证明依赖于相对能量的适当调整。

## 英文摘要

We study the Euler--Riesz system on the torus $\mathbb T^d$, $d=2,3$: the compressible Euler equations with barotropic pressure $p(\varrho)=a\varrho^γ$ ($γ>1$, $a>0$), coupled to a repulsive nonlocal force ($\approx \varrho \nabla_x K \ast \varrho$) with Riesz kernel $K(x)\propto|x|^{β-d}$ of order $β\in(0,2)$. Since $K$ is the kernel of the inverse fractional Laplacian $(-Δ)^{-β/2}$, we recast the force through the Caffarelli--Silvestre extension as the trace of a local stress tensor, replacing the nonlocal interaction by a local identity in one extra variable. For a repulsive kernel the total energy is coercive, and we use this to introduce a notion of global-in-time \emph{dissipative solution} for arbitrarily large finite-energy data. Our main result is weak (measure-valued)--strong uniqueness, for every order $β\in(0,2)$ and every $γ>1$ independently: on any interval on which a strong solution exists, every dissipative solution with the same initial data coincides with it and all defects vanish. The proof rests on a suitable adaptation of relative energy.
