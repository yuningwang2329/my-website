# Burgers equation with a twist: a study on rotational-form equations

- **作者 (Authors)**: Adam Larios
- **来源 (Source)**: Nonlinearity
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-12
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6544/ae8eee)

## 中文摘要

摘要
                  引入了一个新的三维（3D）方程，它的形成类似于伯格斯方程，从3D不可压缩纳维-斯托克斯方程（NSE）开始，消除压力和无散度约束，但消除了伯努利压力，只留下非线性的旋转形式。这会产生一个全局适定的 3D 方程，其能量平衡与 3D NSE 完全相同。此外，我们在模拟中表明系统似乎表现出混沌动力学。在粘性情况下，我们证明了该方程解的全局存在性、唯一性和高阶正则性，除了平滑性之外，对初始数据没有任何限制。在无粘性情况下，局部存在成立，但我们给出了一类具有平滑初始数据的解的示例，这些解在 2D 和 3D 中都在有限时间内形成了奇点。此外，在二维情况下提出了一种新的数值算法，并通过仿真来说明动力学。原始 3D NSE 的全局适定性仍然是一个具有挑战性的开放问题，但希望通过关注旋转项，可以获得新的见解。

## 英文摘要

Abstract
                  A new three-dimensional (3D) equation is introduced, which is formed like Burgers’ equation by starting with the 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes equations (NSE) and eliminating the pressure and the divergence-free constraint, but instead the Bernoulli pressure is eliminated, leaving only the rotational form of the nonlinearity. This results in a globally well-posed 3D equation which has exactly the same energy balance as the 3D NSE. Moreover, we show in simulations that the system seems to exhibit chaotic dynamics. In the viscous case, we prove the global existence, uniqueness, and higher-order regularity of solutions to this equation with no restriction on the initial data other than smoothness. In the inviscid case, local existence holds, but we give an example of a class of solutions with smooth initial data that develop a singularity in finite time in both 2D and 3D. Moreover, a new numerical algorithm is presented in the 2D case, and simulations are included to illustrate the dynamics. Global well-posedness for the original 3D NSE remains a challenging open problem, but it is hoped that by focusing on the rotational term, new insight may be gained.
