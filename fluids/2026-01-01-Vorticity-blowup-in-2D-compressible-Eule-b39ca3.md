# Vorticity blowup in 2D compressible Euler equations

- **作者 (Authors)**: Jiajie Chen, Giorgio Cialdea, Steve Shkoller, Vlad Vicol
- **来源 (Source)**: Duke Math. J.
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-01-01
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://doi.org/10.1215/00127094-2025-0061)

## 中文摘要

我们证明了具有平滑、局部和非空初始数据的二维可压缩欧拉方程的平滑解的有限时间涡度爆炸。涡旋爆发发生在第一个奇点时，并伴随着轴对称内爆，其中旋流速度享有完全稳定性，而不是有限余维稳定性。

---

## 英文摘要

We prove finite-time vorticity blowup for smooth solutions of the 2D compressible Euler equations with smooth, localized, and nonvacuous initial data. The vorticity blowup occurs at the time of the first singularity, and is accompanied by an axisymmetric implosion in which the swirl velocity enjoys full stability, as opposed to finite codimension stability.
