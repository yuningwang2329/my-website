# The IBVP for the Navier-Stokes equations in the half-space in a class of weighted Lebesgue spaces

- **作者 (Authors)**: Angelica Pia Di Feola, Vittorio Pane
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-05
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04621v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究半空间中纳维-斯托克斯方程的初始边值问题，初始数据属于合适的加权勒贝格空间。更准确地说，我们考虑与权重函数相关联的加权勒贝格空间，权重函数定义为有限多个固定点的距离幂的乘积。该框架是 A. P. Di Feola 和 V. Pane 在之前的工作（J. Math. Anal. Appl. 558 (2026), 130390）中引入的，用于研究 Stokes 系统的初始边值问题。本文完成了该分析，同时将 Maremonti 和 Pane (J. Math. Fluid Mech. 27 (2025), Art. 2) 针对 Navier-Stokes Cauchy 问题获得的结果推广到初始边值问题。我们证明了平滑解的存在性（局部）和唯一性，并推导出 $L^q$ 估计，其中 $q>n$，以及速度场的空间渐近行为。

## 英文摘要

We study the initial-boundary value problem for the Navier-Stokes equations in the half-space with initial data belonging to a suitable weighted Lebesgue space. More precisely, we consider a weighted Lebesgue space associated with a weight function defined as a product of powers of the distances from finitely many fixed points. This framework was introduced in a previous work by A. P. Di Feola and V. Pane (J. Math. Anal. Appl. 558 (2026), 130390) for the study of the initial-boundary value problem for the Stokes system. The present paper completes that analysis and, at the same time, generalizes to the initial-boundary value problem the results obtained by Maremonti and Pane (J. Math. Fluid Mech. 27 (2025), Art. 2) for the Navier-Stokes Cauchy problem. We prove the existence (local) and uniqueness of a smooth solution and derive $L^q$-estimates, with $q>n$, together with the spatial asymptotic behavior of the velocity field.
