# Dissipative measure-valued solutions and weak--strong uniqueness for a viscous Baer--Nunziato system with pressure relaxation

- **作者 (Authors)**: Nilasis Chaudhuri, Milan Pokorný, Ewelina Zatorska
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-13
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13348v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究了有界三维域中两种正压可压缩流体的粘性单速度 Baer-Nunziato 系统。与仅通过代数平衡约束传输或确定体积分数的模型相比，它满足由相之间的压力间隙驱动的微分闭合。对于任意有限能量初始数据和绝热指数 $γ^\pm>1$，我们构造了全局时间耗散测度值解，并证明了耗散测度值相对于具有相同初始数据的任何足够规则的解的强唯一性。主要困难是压力弛豫源在体积分数消失时的奇异和非连续行为、相关的浓度缺陷以及标准热力学相对能量相对于体积分数缺乏直接矫顽力。这些可以通过端点截止参数和与重正化体积分数方程耦合的增强相对能量来解决。

## 英文摘要

We study a viscous one-velocity Baer--Nunziato system for two barotropic compressible fluids in a bounded three-dimensional domain. In contrast with models in which the volume fraction is merely transported or determined by an algebraic equilibrium constraint, it satisfies a differential closure driven by the pressure gap between the phases. For arbitrary finite-energy initial data and adiabatic exponents $γ^\pm>1$, we construct global-in-time dissipative measure-valued solutions and prove dissipative measure-valued--strong uniqueness relative to any sufficiently regular solution with the same initial data. The principal difficulties are the singular and non-continuous behavior of the pressure-relaxation source at vanishing volume fractions, the associated concentration defects, and the lack of direct coercivity of the standard thermodynamic relative energy with respect to the volume fraction. These are resolved by an endpoint cutoff argument and an augmented relative energy coupled to the renormalized volume-fraction equation.
