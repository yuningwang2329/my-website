# Anomalous dissipation via spontaneous stochasticity with a two-dimensional autonomous velocity field

- **作者 (Authors)**: Carl Johan Peter Johansson, Massimo Sorella
- **来源 (Source)**: Duke Math. J.
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-01-01
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://doi.org/10.1215/00127094-2025-0067)

## 中文摘要

我们研究了无源标量背景下的反常耗散，并在 Cα 中构造了一个二维自主无散度速度场（其中 αε(0,1) 任意但固定），它表现出反常耗散。我们的证明采用波动耗散公式，它将自发随机性与反常耗散联系起来。因此，我们通过证明随机轨迹的方差在零噪声极限下保持为正来解决反常耗散问题。基于这个结果，我们回答了 Bruè 和 De Lellis 中有关强制三维纳维-斯托克斯方程的反常耗散的问题 2.2 和问题 2.3。

## 英文摘要

We study anomalous dissipation in the context of passive scalars and we construct a two-dimensional autonomous divergence-free velocity field in Cα (with α∈(0,1) arbitrary but fixed) which exhibits anomalous dissipation. Our proof employs the fluctuation-dissipation formula, which links spontaneous stochasticity with anomalous dissipation. Therefore, we address the issue of anomalous dissipation by showing that the variance of stochastic trajectories, in the zero noise limit, remains positive. Based on this result, we answer Questions 2.2 and Question 2.3 in Bruè and De Lellis regarding anomalous dissipation for the forced three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations.
