# On dynamic stability of energetically stable equilibria of the Navier-Stokes-Korteweg flows

- **作者 (Authors)**: Yoshikazu Giga, Naoto Kajiwara, Kazuyuki Tsuda
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-06
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05654v1)

## 中文摘要

我们考虑有界域或周期单元中的 Navier-Stokes-Korteweg 方程。本文考虑的压力相对于密度可能不是单调的，因此存在允许两相的非恒定平衡。使用简单的希尔伯特空间框架，我们证明如果孤立平衡是能量稳定且非简并的，那么当空间维数小于或等于三时，它在等温纳维-斯托克斯-科特韦格流下呈指数稳定。对于非孤立情况，我们证明了能量稳定平衡附近的全局时间解可能以指数速度快速收敛到另一个平衡。不强加任何关于均衡的小假设。为了证明，我们应用 J. Prüss、M. Wilke 和 G. Simonett (2013) 提出的（广义）稳定性原理。

## 英文摘要

We consider the Navier-Stokes-Korteweg equations in a bounded domain or a periodic cell. The pressure considered in this paper may not be monotone with respect to the density so that there exist non-constant equilibria allowing two-phases. Using a simple Hilbert space framework, we prove that if an isolated equilibrium is energetically stable and non- degenerate, it is exponentially stable under the isothermal Navier-Stokes-Korteweg flows when the space dimension is less than or equal to three. For non-isolated case, we prove that a global-in-time solution near an energetically stable equilibrium converges to possibly another equilibrium exponentially fast. No smallness assumptions on equilibria are imposed. For the proof we apply a (generalized) stability principle due to J. Prüss, M. Wilke and G. Simonett (2013).
