# Couette-Taylor instabilities in the small gap regime: the very counter-rotating case

- **作者 (Authors)**: Dongfen Bian, Gérard Iooss
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-11
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10951v1)

## 中文摘要

在本文中，我们研究了两个旋转圆柱体之间的粘性流体在小间隙、慢速旋转速率、高雷诺数状态下的库埃特-泰勒不稳定性，重点关注非常反向旋转的情况 $μ< μ_c \approx -0.8$，其中主要不稳定性是非轴对称的。从纳维-斯托克斯方程出发，我们推导了一个极限系统，该系统捕获主阶动力学并计算临界泰勒数 $T_c(μ)$ 以及临界轴向和方位波数。接近临界点时，弱非线性行为由两个耦合的复杂 Ginzburg-Landau 方程组控制。该振幅系统的所有系数（包括三次非线性项）均根据线性化本征函数和相关伴随问题进行数值计算。简化方程允许螺旋波（轴向和方位角方向传播）和带状波（轴向站立，方位角方向传播），并讨论了它们的存在性和稳定性准则。我们还研究了更奇特的空间调制解决方案，以满足三阶动力系统，其完整分类仍然是一个开放的挑战。

## 英文摘要

In this paper, we study the Couette-Taylor instability of a viscous fluid between two rotating cylinders in the small-gap, slow rescaled rotation rate, high Reynolds number regime, focusing on the very counter-rotating case $μ< μ_c \approx -0.8$ where the primary instability is non-axisymmetric. Starting from the Navier-Stokes equations, we derive a limit system that captures the leading-order dynamics and compute the critical Taylor number $T_c(μ)$ together with the critical axial and azimuthal wavenumbers. Near criticality, the weakly nonlinear behaviour is governed by a system of two coupled complex Ginzburg-Landau equations. All coefficients of this amplitude system including the cubic nonlinear terms are evaluated numerically from the linearised eigenfunctions and the associated adjoint problem. The reduced equations admit helicoidal waves (travelling in both the axial and azimuthal directions) and ribbon waves (standing axially, travelling azimuthally), and their existence and stability criteria are discussed. We also examine more exotic spatially modulated solutions that satisfy a third-order dynamical system, whose complete classification remains an open challenge.
