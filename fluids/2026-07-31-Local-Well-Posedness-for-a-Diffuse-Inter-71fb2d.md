# Local Well-Posedness for a Diffuse Interface Model for Two-Phase Flows from Mixture Theory

- **作者 (Authors)**: Helmut Abels, Harald Garcke, Julia Wittmann
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-07-31
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29298v1)

## 中文摘要

为最近提出的描述不可压缩两相流的扩散界面模型建立了局部适定性。该结果构成了对 10 名 Eikelder 等人提出的模型的首次分析研究。用于宏观上不混溶、粘性、不可压缩且密度无与伦比的二元混合物的运动。与基于单一平均速度的经典扩散界面模型相比，该模型是在混合理论框架内推导的，为每个相分配了自己的动量和质量平衡，从而形成了两个耦合的纳维-斯托克斯方程和两个质量传递方程的系统。适定结果的证明采用定点策略，其主要难点在于相关线性化系统主体部分的分析。

## 英文摘要

Local-in-time well-posedness is established for a recently proposed diffuse interface model describing incompressible two-phase flows. The result constitutes the first analytical study of a model introduced by ten Eikelder et al. for the motion of a binary mixture of macroscopically immiscible, viscous, incompressible fluids with unmatched densities. In contrast to classic diffuse interface models based on a single mean velocity, this model is derived within the framework of mixture theory, assigning each phase its own momentum and mass balance, which results in a system of two coupled Navier--Stokes equations and two mass transport equations. The proof of the well-posedness result uses a fixed-point strategy, where the main difficulty lies in the analysis of the principal part of the associated linearized system.
