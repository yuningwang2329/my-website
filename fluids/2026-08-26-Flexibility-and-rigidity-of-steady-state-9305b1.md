# Flexibility and rigidity of steady states of the two-dimensional Euler equations in an infinite channel

- **作者 (Authors)**: Yupei Huang, Chunjing Xie, Chilin Zhang
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-26
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25859v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究无限通道中二维不可压缩欧拉方程的稳定解，其远场极限是均匀非停滞剪切流。在光滑范畴中，对于一大类规定的远场剪切剖面，通过最小-最大方法构造流函数半线性椭圆方程的二维解，存在非剪切稳态。在解析类别中，我们建立了解析稳态的比较原理，并且我们表明，对于解析均匀非停滞剪切剖面的密集族，具有指定远场的每个解析稳态本身都必须是剪切流。特别是，远场剪切剖面在平滑类别中表现出灵活性，但在解析类别中表现出刚性。此外，在存在允许分析范畴中的灵活性的分析剪切剖面的意义上，致密刚度是尖锐的。

## 英文摘要

We study steady solutions to the two-dimensional incompressible Euler equations in an infinite channel, whose far-field limits are uniformly non-stagnant shear flows. In the smooth category,for a broad class of prescribed far-field shear profiles, non-shear steady states exist via the construction of two-dimensional solutions of the semilinear elliptic equations of stream function by the min--max method. In the analytic category, we establish a comparison principle for the analytic steady states and we show for a dense family of analytic uniformly non-stagnant shear profiles, every analytic steady state with the prescribed far field must itself be a shear flow. In particular, there are far-field shear profiles which exhibit flexibility in the smooth category but rigidity in the analytic category. Furthermore, the dense rigidity is sharp in the sense that there exists analytic shear profile which admits flexibility in the analytic category.
