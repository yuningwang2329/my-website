# Finite-time singularity formation for angle-crested water waves

- **作者 (Authors)**: Diego Córdoba, Alberto Enciso, Nastasia Grubic
- **来源 (Source)**: Duke Math. J.
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-01-01
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://doi.org/10.1215/00127094-2025-0063)

## 中文摘要

我们表明，水波系统在加权索博列夫空间中局部处于良好状态，允许与角的界面。不需要对称性假设。这些奇异点不是刚性的：如果初始界面呈现角点，它仍然是角点，但通常其角度会发生变化。利用根据我们的先验能量估计得出的角点附近流体渐近行为的表征，我们证明了这些空间中初始数据的存在，在这些空间中，流体在有限时间内变得奇异。

## 英文摘要

We show that the water waves system is locally well-posed in weighted Sobolev spaces which allow for interfaces with corners. No symmetry assumptions are required. These singular points are not rigid: If the initial interface exhibits a corner, it remains a corner but generically its angle changes. Using a characterization of the asymptotic behavior of the fluid near a corner that follows from our a priori energy estimates, we show the existence of initial data in these spaces for which the fluid becomes singular in finite time.
