# Expanding solutions to the compressible Navier-Stokes equations with degenerate viscosities in spherical symmetry: global existence and inviscid limit

- **作者 (Authors)**: Shuying Hu, Zhouping Xin, Yuan Yuan
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-26
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25394v1)

## 中文摘要

本文致力于研究球对称下具有密度相关粘度的等熵可压缩纳维-斯托克斯方程的真空自由边界问题的强解，该方程模拟了真空包围的等熵可压缩粘性流的运动。当初始数据是绝热指数 $γ>1$ 的扩展仿射解的小扰动且粘度系数与 $ρ^γ$ 成比例（其中 $ρ$ 是流体密度）时，我们构造一类扩展全局解。此外，当粘性系数趋于零时，扰动解收敛于可压缩欧拉方程的解，且粘性系数具有显式收敛率。

## 英文摘要

This paper is devoted to studying the strong solutions to the vacuum free boundary problem for the isentropic compressible Navier-Stokes equations with density-dependent viscosities under spherical symmetry, which models the motions of isentropic compressible viscous flows surrounded by vacuum. We construct a class of expanding global solutions when the initial data is a small perturbation of the expanding affine solutions for the adiabatic exponent $γ>1$ and the viscosity coefficients proportional to $ρ^γ$, with $ρ$ being the fluid density. In addition, when the viscosity coefficients tend to zero, the perturbed solutions are proved to converge to the solutions to the compressible Euler equations with an explicit converging rate of viscosity coefficients.
