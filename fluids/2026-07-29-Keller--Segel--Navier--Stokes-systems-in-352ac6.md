# Keller--Segel--Navier--Stokes systems involving general sensitivities with Signal-Dependent Power-Law Decay

- **作者 (Authors)**: Sukjung Hwang, Jaewook Ahn
- **来源 (Source)**: J. Differ. Equ.
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-07-29
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022039626005644?dgcid=rss_sd_all)

## 中文摘要

本文研究了具有张量值趋化敏感性$S(x,n,c)$的二维Keller--Segel--Navier--Stokes系统。在信号相关的功率衰减条件下 $|S(x,n,c)| \le s_0 (s_1+c)^{-\gamma}$，我们建立了流体耦合（$\gamma > 1/2$）和无流体（$\gamma > 0$）系统经典解的全局存在性和时间一致有界性。该证明依赖于一系列局部能量估计，包括信号浓度加权梯度的$L^{2}_{\rm loc}$-小度，以克服信号产生和流体传输产生的数学困难。此外，在灵敏度张量的特定结构假设下，我们证明无流体系统的解以指数方式收敛到空间均匀稳态。为此，我们建立了一个涉及 H\"older 范数的插值不等式，它具有独立的利益，并且似乎具有广泛的应用。​

## 英文摘要

This paper investigates a two-dimensional Keller--Segel--Navier--Stokes system with a tensor-valued chemotactic sensitivity $S(x,n,c)$. Under a signal-dependent power-decay condition $|S(x,n,c)| \le s_0 (s_1+c)^{-\gamma}$, we establish the global existence and uniform-in-time boundedness of classical solutions for both fluid-coupled ($\gamma &gt; 1/2$) and fluid-free ($\gamma &gt; 0$) systems. The proof relies on a sequence of localized energy estimates, including the $L^{2}_{\rm loc}$-smallness of the weighted gradient of the signal concentration, to overcome the mathematical difficulties arising from signal production and fluid transport. Furthermore, under specific structural assumptions on the sensitivity tensor, we prove that solutions of the fluid-free system converge exponentially to the spatially homogeneous steady state. To this end, we establish an interpolation inequality involving the H\"older norm, which is of independent interest and seems to have broad applications.​
