# Stable determination of time-dependent coefficients in a reaction-diffusion-convection system

- **作者 (Authors)**: Rahul Bhardwaj, Parveen Kumar
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-07
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07190v1)

## 中文摘要

在这篇手稿中，我们研究了 $\mathbb{R}^{1+n}$, $n\geq 2$ 有界域中反应扩散对流系统的逆边值问题。我们的目标是获得稳定性估计，用于从狄利克雷到诺依曼图表示的边界测量中确定时间相关的对流系数和矩阵值势。我们考虑部分数据设置，其中测量仅在稍微超过边界一半的横向边界的子集上可用。我们首先建立相关初始边值问题的适定性。随后，通过将卡尔曼估计与合适的几何光学解决方案相结合，我们得出了未知系数的稳定性估计。更准确地说，我们根据部分狄利克雷到诺伊曼图的知识证明了时间相关对流系数的双对数 ($\log$-$\log$) 稳定性估计。然后利用该稳定性结果来恢复矩阵值势，从而产生零阶系数的三重对数 ($\log$-$\log$-$\log$) 稳定性估计。

## 英文摘要

In this manuscript, we investigate an inverse boundary value problem for a reaction-diffusion-convection system in a bounded domain of $\mathbb{R}^{1+n}$, $n\geq 2$. We aim to obtain a stability estimate for determining the time-dependent convection coefficient and matrix-valued potential from boundary measurements represented by the Dirichlet-to-Neumann map. We consider a partial data setting in which the measurements are available only on a subset of the lateral boundary that slightly exceeds one-half of the boundary. We first establish the well-posedness of the associated initial-boundary value problem. Subsequently, by combining Carleman estimates with suitable geometric optics solutions, we derive stability estimates for the unknown coefficients. More precisely, we prove a double logarithmic ($\log$-$\log$) stability estimate for the time-dependent convection coefficient from the knowledge of the partial Dirichlet-to-Neumann map. This stability result is then employed to recover the matrix-valued potential, yielding a triple logarithmic ($\log$-$\log$-$\log$) stability estimate for the zeroth-order coefficient.
