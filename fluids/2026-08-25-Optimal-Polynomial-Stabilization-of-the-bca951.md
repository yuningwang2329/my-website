# Optimal Polynomial Stabilization of the Linearized Periodic Whitham--Boussinesq System

- **作者 (Authors)**: Roberto de A. Capistrano Filho, William Artiles Roqueta
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-25
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25226v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究了一维环面上线性化周期 Whitham-Boussinesq 系统的稳定性。我们在自然能量空间中建立了保守和阻尼动力学的适定性，并描述了保守发生器的谱结构，其频率在高频下表现出 $|k|^{1/2}$ 量级的次线性增长。然后，我们证明了阻尼半群的强稳定性，并获得了谱参数线性增长的高频解析估计。在真正的局部阻尼下，一系列高频准模提供了匹配的下限，并表明这种解析增长是最佳的。根据 Borichev--Tomilov 定理 [5]，我们推导出生成元域上半群的 $t^{-1}$ 衰减率，对应于能量的 $t^{-2}$ 衰减率。在相同的局部化假设下，这些多项式衰减率是最优的。

## 英文摘要

We study the stabilization of the linearized periodic Whitham--Boussinesq system on the one-dimensional torus. We establish the well-posedness of the conservative and damped dynamics in the natural energy space and describe the spectral structure of the conservative generator, whose frequencies exhibit sublinear growth of order $|k|^{1/2}$ at high frequency. We then prove strong stability of the damped semigroup and obtain a high-frequency resolvent estimate with linear growth in the spectral parameter. Under genuinely localized damping, a family of high-frequency quasimodes provides the matching lower bound and shows that this resolvent growth is optimal. By the Borichev--Tomilov theorem [5], we deduce a $t^{-1}$ decay rate for the semigroup on the domain of the generator, corresponding to a $t^{-2}$ decay rate for the energy. Under the same localization assumption, these polynomial decay rates are optimal.
