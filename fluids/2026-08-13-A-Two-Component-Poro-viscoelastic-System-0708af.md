# A Two-Component Poro-viscoelastic System for Fibre-Reinforced Hydrogels: Analysis and Homogenization

- **作者 (Authors)**: Michael Eden, Hari Shankar Mahato
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-13
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13051v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究了组织工程支架中使用的纤维增强水凝胶（FIH）建模中出现的耦合粘孔弹性系统的多尺度行为。该复合材料由周期性纤维支架和饱和间隙空间的水凝胶相组成，该支架由准静态线弹性控制，该水凝胶相被建模为通过 Kelvin-Voigt 结构阻尼增强的 Biot 线性多孔弹性介质。这两个相通过其共享界面上的位移和牵引力的连续性来耦合。纤维支架和水凝胶相都是连接的，因此机械力可以通过复合材料传递，并且间质液可以直接流过水凝胶网络。   从微观（$\varepsilon$-尺度）模型开始，我们得出统一的先验估计，并通过 Rothe 时间离散论证为标准 Biot 流体含量 $η=0$ 的情况和粘性流体含量 $η=αδ>0$ 的情况建立适定性。然后，我们使用周期性展开在 $\varepsilon \ 到 0$ 的限制下执行严格的两尺度均质化。除了通常的有效弹性、存储、耦合和渗透系数之外，均质本构定律还包含由微观粘弹性松弛产生的非局部时间记忆项。所有有效系数和记忆内核都根据微观几何形状和材料参数进行明确表征。

## 英文摘要

We study the multiscale behavior of a coupled visco-poroelastic system arising in the modelling of fibre-reinforced hydrogels (FIHs) used in tissue engineering scaffolds. The composite material consists of a periodic fibre scaffold, which is governed by quasi-static linear elasticity, and a hydrogel phase saturating the interstitial space, which is modelled as a Biot linear poroelastic medium enhanced with Kelvin--Voigt structural damping. The two phases are coupled through continuity of displacement and traction across their shared interface. Both the fibre scaffold and the hydrogel phase are connected, so that mechanical forces can be transmitted through the composite and interstitial fluid can flow directly through the hydrogel network.   Starting from a microscopic ($\varepsilon$-scale) model, we derive uniform a-priori estimates and establish well-posedness via a Rothe time-discretisation argument for both the case of standard Biot fluid content $η=0$ and the case of viscous fluid content $η=αδ>0$. We then perform a rigorous two-scale homogenization in the limit $\varepsilon \to 0$ using periodic unfolding. In addition to the usual effective elasticity, storage, coupling, and permeability coefficients, the homogenized constitutive laws contain nonlocal-in-time memory terms generated by the microscopic viscoelastic relaxation. All effective coefficients and memory kernels are explicitly characterized in terms of the microscale geometry and material parameters.
