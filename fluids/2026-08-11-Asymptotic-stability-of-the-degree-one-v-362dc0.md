# Asymptotic stability of the degree-one vortex in the abelian Yang-Mills-Higgs model: Linear Theory

- **作者 (Authors)**: Jonas Luhrmann, José M. Palacios, Fabio Pusateri, Wilhelm Schlag, Sohrab Shahshahani
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-11
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10609v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究了自对偶耦合下 $(1+2)$ 维阿贝尔 Yang-Mills-Higgs 模型的一级涡旋附近的线性化动力学，仅限于正交规范中的等变扰动。线性化算子是径向 $L^2_{\mathrm{rad}}(\mathbb{R}^2;\mathbb{R}^4)$ 上的自伴矩阵薛定谔算子 $\mathbf{M}$ ，具有连续谱 $[1,\infty)$ 和唯一间隙特征值 $λ^2 \in (0,1)$ 处的二维内模，如我们三篇论文系列的第一部分中所建立的基态涡旋的渐近稳定性.在本系列的第二部分中，我们证明了第三部分应用中 $\mathbf{M}$ 的线性估计。具体来说，我们证明了色散和局部能量衰减估计，以及传递关系，这使我们能够在第三部分的非线性分析中实现关于平坦 Klein-Gordon 算子的时空共振方法。   在我们的方法中，证明 $\mathbf{M}$ 线性估计的引擎是与 $\mathbf{M}$ 相关的扭曲傅里叶变换。扭曲傅里叶变换的构造以及对底层广义本征函数的详细分析占据了本文的前半部分。为此，我们利用 $\mathbf{M}$ 的超对称分解和超对称伙伴算子的对角结构，将问题与两个强奇异标量半线薛定谔算子的 Weyl-Titchmarsh 理论联系起来。

## 英文摘要

We study the linearized dynamics near the degree-one vortex of the $(1+2)$-dimensional abelian Yang-Mills-Higgs model at the self-dual coupling, restricted to equivariant perturbations in the orthogonal gauge. The linearized operator is a selfadjoint matrix Schrödinger operator $\mathbf{M}$ on radial $L^2_{\mathrm{rad}}(\mathbb{R}^2;\mathbb{R}^4)$ with continuous spectrum $[1,\infty)$ and a two-dimensional internal mode at a unique gap eigenvalue $λ^2 \in (0,1)$, as established in Part I of our three-paper series on asymptotic stability of the ground state vortex. In this second part of the series, we prove linear estimates for $\mathbf{M}$ for applications in Part III. Specifically, we prove dispersive and local-energy decay estimates, as well as a transference relation which allows us to implement the space-time resonance method with respect to the flat Klein-Gordon operator in the nonlinear analysis in Part III.   The engine for proving linear estimates for $\mathbf{M}$ in our approach is the distorted Fourier transform associated with $\mathbf{M}$. The construction of the distorted Fourier transform together with a detailed analysis of the underlying generalized eigenfunctions occupy the first half of this paper. For this we exploit the super-symmetric factorization of $\mathbf{M}$, and the diagonal structure of the super-symmetric partner operator, to relate the problem to the Weyl-Titchmarsh theory of two strongly singular scalar half-line Schrödinger operators.
