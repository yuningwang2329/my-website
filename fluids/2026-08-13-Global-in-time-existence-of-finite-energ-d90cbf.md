# Global-in-time existence of finite energy weak solutions to a relaxed Navier-Stokes-Korteweg model

- **作者 (Authors)**: Florian Oschmann, Florian Wendt
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-13
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12969v1)

## 中文摘要

我们考虑可压缩 Navier-Stokes-Korteweg 系统的抛物线松弛公式，并证明相关初始边界值问题的有限能量弱解的全局时间存在性。我们的证明基于三级近似方案、有效粘性通量的弱紧性性质以及抛物线正则性估计。我们的结果适用于各种非单调压力函数，并将可压缩纳维-斯托克斯方程的已知相应结果推广到松弛系统。

---

## 英文摘要

We consider a parabolic relaxation formulation of the compressible Navier-Stokes-Korteweg system and prove the global-in-time existence of finite energy weak solutions to the associated initial-boundary-value-problem. Our proof is based on a three-level approximation scheme, a weak compactness property of the effective viscous flux, and parabolic regularity estimates. Our result holds for a broad variety of non-monotone pressure functions and generalizes the corresponding results known for the compressible Navier-Stokes equations to the relaxation system.
