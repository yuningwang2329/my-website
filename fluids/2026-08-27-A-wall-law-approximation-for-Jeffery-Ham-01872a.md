# A wall-law approximation for Jeffery-Hamel flows in rough wedges

- **作者 (Authors)**: Zijin Li, Xinghong Pan
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-27
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26736v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究了无界楔中的二维稳态纳维-斯托克斯方程，其角边界具有小的粗糙扰动。相应直楔中的 Jeffery-Hamel 流被视为有效背景流。在合适的小通量条件下，我们证明了弱解的存在，并建立了$O(ε^2)$阶的$H^1$型能量误差估计。对于足够小的楔角，我们进一步推导加权估计并将平方 $L^2$ 误差改进为 $O(ε^3)$。

---

## 英文摘要

We study the two-dimensional stationary Navier-Stokes equations in an unbounded wedge with small rough perturbations of its angular boundaries. The Jeffery-Hamel flow in the corresponding straight wedge is taken as the effective background flow. Under a suitable small-flux condition, we prove the existence of weak solutions and establish an $H^1$-type energy error estimate of order $O(ε^2)$. For sufficiently small wedge angles, we further derive weighted estimates and improve the squared $L^2$-error to $O(ε^3)$.
