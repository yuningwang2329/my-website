# On countable subsets of solutions of nonlinear higher-order ODEs and elliptic PDEs with indefinite operators

- **作者 (Authors)**: Pablo Alvarez-Caudevilla, Jonathan D. Ev ans, Victor A. Galaktionov
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-03
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02532v1)

## 中文摘要

通过粘合/匹配论证，可以从反应扩散、薄膜和动力系统 (DS) 理论中获得具有不定非强制算子的高阶非线性 ODE 和椭圆 PDE 解的可数子集。我们特别研究了 $\mathbb{R}$ 中的一些经典和拟线性简并常微分方程，其边界条件为无穷大 $F(\infty)=0$，具有非奇非线性，例如 $$ \begin{matrix} F^{(4)} =-F+F^2,\, \,\, F^{(4)}=-F+F^2{\rm e}^{F-1}, \, (|F''|F'')''=-F + F^2, \\ F^{(4)} =-|F|F+F^2, \,\,\,F^{(4)} =-F^3+F^4, \,\,\, F^{(6)}=F-F^2, \end{matrix} $$ 等，以及具有奇数和非光滑非线性的方程，例如$$F^{(4)}=-F+F^3, \,F^{(4)}=-F-(|F|F-F)'', \,\, F^{(4)}=- \frac F{\sqrt{|F|}}-(F^3-F)'',\,\mbox{etc.}$$ 其中一些 ODE 是哈密顿量，并在 DS 理论中进行了详细研究。基于非线性算子、椭圆偏微分方程和变分理论，$\mathbb{R}^N$、$F(\infty)=0$中的相关多调和椭圆方程，如$$Δ^2 F=-F+F^2、\quad Δ^2 F=-F -Δ(F^2-F)、\quad Δ^3 F=F-F^2、\quad \mbox{etc.};$$ 也被证明可以接受无数的解决方案。对于此类具有非奇泛函的方程，保证一系列临界点存在的 Lusternik-Schnirel'man (L--S) 属/范畴变分理论并不适用。这些常微分方程和椭圆偏微分方程（例如，在径向设置中）被证明允许至少两个基本可数族 ${\mathcal F}_{1,2}$ 的正主解，与两个周期轨道 $Γ_{\rm max/min}$ 连接。通过将来自$Γ$的任意有限样本的指数衰减尾部粘合在一起而获得的模式形成任意复杂度的$\mathbb{R}^4$中同宿的可数子集。

## 英文摘要

Countable subsets of solutions of higher-order nonlinear ODEs and elliptic PDEs with indefinite non-coercive operators from the reaction-diffusion, thin film and dynamical system (DS) theories are obtained via a gluing/matching argument. In particular we study some classic and quasilinear degenerate ODEs in $\mathbb{R}$, with boundary conditions at infinity $F(\infty)=0$, with non-odd nonlinearities such as   $$ \begin{matrix} F^{(4)} =-F+F^2,\, \,\, F^{(4)}=-F+F^2{\rm e}^{F-1}, \, (|F''|F'')''=-F + F^2, \\ F^{(4)} =-|F|F+F^2, \,\,\,F^{(4)} =-F^3+F^4, \,\,\, F^{(6)}=F-F^2, \end{matrix} $$ etc, as well as equations with odd and non-smooth nonlinearities like   $$F^{(4)}=-F+F^3, \,F^{(4)}=-F-(|F|F-F)'', \,\, F^{(4)}=- \frac F{\sqrt{|F|}}-(F^3-F)'',\,\mbox{etc.}$$ Some of these ODEs are Hamiltonian and were studied in detail in the DS theory. On the basis of nonlinear operators, elliptic PDEs and variational theory, related polyharmonic elliptic equations in $\mathbb{R}^N$, $F(\infty)=0$, such as $$Δ^2 F=-F+F^2, \quad Δ^2 F=-F -Δ(F^2-F), \quad Δ^3 F=F-F^2, \quad \mbox{etc.};$$ are also shown to admit countable families of solutions. For such equations with non-odd functionals associated Lusternik--Schnirel'man (L--S) genus/category variational theory guaranteeing existence of a sequence of critical points does not apply. These ODEs and elliptic PDEs (e.g., in the radial setting) are shown to admit at least two basic countable families ${\mathcal F}_{1,2}$ of positively dominant solutions connected with two periodic orbits $Γ_{\rm max/min}$. Patterns obtained by gluing together via exponentially decaying tails of arbitrary finite samples from $Γ$'s form a countable subset of homoclinics in $\mathbb{R}^4$ of an arbitrary complexity.
