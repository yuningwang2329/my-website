# Forced self-similar solutions to the stationary Navier--Stokes equations in a half-space

- **作者 (Authors)**: Yun Wang, Chunjing Xie, Shaoheng Zhang
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-26
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25432v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究了由轴对称 (-3) 齐次外力驱动的半空间中具有无滑移边界条件的稳态纳维-斯托克斯方程的轴对称自相似解。如果单位球上的力的切向旋度足够小，我们证明存在唯一的小解；当力无涡流时，解自动无涡流且唯一。对于无旋流外力$\boldsymbol{F}$，我们引入缩放参数$λ$并考虑具有力$λ\boldsymbol{F}$的系统；我们证明在包含零的开区间中，$λ$ 的解精确存在。同样的方法扩展到具有无滑移边界条件的实心锥体，其中较窄的开口角度允许在较大的外力下存在解。

---

## 英文摘要

We study axisymmetric self-similar solutions to the stationary Navier--Stokes equations in the half-space with the no-slip boundary condition, driven by an axisymmetric (-3)-homogeneous external force. If the tangential curl of the force on the unit sphere is sufficiently small, we prove the existence of a unique small solution; when the force is swirl-free, the solution is automatically swirl-free and unique. For a swirl-free external force $\boldsymbol{F}$, we introduce a scaling parameter $λ$ and consider the system with force $λ\boldsymbol{F}$; we prove that solutions exist precisely for $λ$ in an open interval containing zero. The same approach extends to solid cones with the no-slip boundary condition, where narrower opening angles allow the existence of solutions under larger external forces.
