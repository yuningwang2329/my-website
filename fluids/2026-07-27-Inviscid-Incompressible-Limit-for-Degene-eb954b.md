# Inviscid Incompressible Limit for Degenerate Compressible Navier-Stokes Equations on Expanding Domains

- **作者 (Authors)**: Tong Tang, Emil Wiedemann, Lu Zhu
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-07-27

## 中文摘要

我们同时研究了 $3D$ 简并可压缩纳维-斯托克斯方程的扩展域的无粘性和低马赫数限制，该方程的粘度取决于密度。从准备不足的数据出发，我们证明极限系统是不可压缩的欧拉系统。   我们的结果扩展了 Feireisl 等人之前的结果。关于恒定粘度纳维-斯托克斯方程，并且是第一个同时为密度相关粘度建立无粘性和不可压缩极限的人。

---

## 英文摘要

We simultaneously investigate the inviscid and Low Mach number limits on expanding domains for the $3D$ degenerate compressible Navier-Stokes equations, whose viscosity depends on density. Starting from ill-prepared data, we show the limit system is the incompressible Euler system.   Our result extends a previous result of Feireisl et al. concerning the constant viscosity Navier-Stokes equations, and is the first to establish the inviscid and incompressible limits at the same time for density-dependent viscosity.
