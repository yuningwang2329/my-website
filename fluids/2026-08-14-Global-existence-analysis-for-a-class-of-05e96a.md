# Global existence analysis for a class of compressible Navier-Stokes-Korteweg equations

- **作者 (Authors)**: Ansgar Jüngel, Flora Philipp
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-14
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14044v1)

## 中文摘要

针对三维环面中的大数据，建立了广泛的 Navier-Stokes-Korteweg 方程的全局弱解的存在，包括作为特例的扩散界面和量子 Navier-Stokes 系统。该模型由具有简并密度相关粘度的可压缩纳维-斯托克斯方程和一般非线性三阶 Korteweg 项组成。存在性证明将能量和 Bresch-Desjardins (BD) 熵不等式提供的先验估计与精心设计的近似方案相结合。该分析的一个关键要素是通过系统分部积分方法获得的与 Korteweg 项相关的新耗散不等式。为了构建解决方案，引入了人工阻力项和量子 Korteweg 正则化，随后通过建立重正化公式将其消除在极限范围内。

## 英文摘要

The existence of global weak solutions to a broad class of Navier-Stokes-Korteweg equations is established for large data in the three-dimensional torus, including the diffuse-interface and quantum Navier-Stokes systems as special cases. The model consists of the compressible Navier-Stokes equations with degenerate density-dependent viscosity and a general nonlinear third-order Korteweg term. The existence proof combines a priori estimates provided by the energy and Bresch-Desjardins (BD) entropy inequalities with a carefully designed approximation scheme. A crucial ingredient of the analysis is a new dissipation inequality associated with the Korteweg term, obtained via the systematic integration-by-parts method. To construct the solutions, artificial drag terms and a quantum Korteweg regularization are introduced, which are subsequently removed in the limit by establishing a renormalized formulation.
