# Long-Wave Stability And Instability Of Periodic Shear Flows For The 2D Navier-Stokes Equations On The $β$-Plane

- **作者 (Authors)**: Robin Ming Chen, Tian Dai, Dehua Wang, Weiqiang Wang
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-07
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06899v1)

## 中文摘要

我们研究了长波区域$β$平面上二维纳维-斯托克斯方程的周期性剪切流的谱稳定性。众所周知，非旋转周期性剪切流通常对于足够长的波扰动是不稳定的。我们证明行星自转可以抑制这种不稳定性：使用基于加藤约简的微扰分析，我们推导了线性算子主特征值的渐近展开式，并获得了剪切剖面、粘度和科里奥利参数方面的显式稳定性准则。在科里奥利效应和长波扰动具有相当大小的临界标度下，该准则将 Yudovich 的经典长波不稳定阈值扩展到旋转流，并揭示了由 $K$ 比率控制的稳定与不稳定之间的急剧转变。这些结果严格说明了粘度、剪切力和旋转如何相互竞争来确定 $β$ 平面上的长波稳定性。

## 英文摘要

We study the spectral stability of periodic shear flows for the two-dimensional Navier--Stokes equations on the $β$-plane in the long-wave regime. It is known that non-rotating periodic shear flows are generically unstable to sufficiently long-wave perturbations. We show that planetary rotation can suppress this instability: using a perturbative analysis based on Kato's reduction, we derive an asymptotic expansion for the principal eigenvalue of the linearized operator and obtain an explicit stability criterion in terms of the shear profile, the viscosity, and the Coriolis parameter. Under the critical scaling where the Coriolis effect and the long-wave perturbation are of comparable size, this criterion extends Yudovich's classical long-wave instability threshold to rotating flows and reveals a sharp transition between stability and instability governed by the ratio $K$. These results give a rigorous account of how viscosity, shear, and rotation compete to determine long-wave stability on the $β$-plane.
