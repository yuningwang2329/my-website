## 英文摘要

In this paper, we study the hydrodynamic limit of the Vlasov-Poisson-Boltzmann system for a gas mixture in the whole space (x ∈R3) with the potential range of γ ∈ (−3,1].Within the framework of the interplay established by Guo and Jang [15], we construct a new weight function to estimate the remainder term in four different cases regarding the potential γ. Here, the particle masses mA,mB &gt; 0 and their charges eA,eB can be given arbitrarily. ​
