## 英文摘要

Abstract
                  
                    We show that jets of initial data can be approximated up to arbitrary order by finite-gap solutions for classes of so-called BKM systems of PDEs introduced by Bolsinov–Konyaev–Matveev, which include classical PDEs such as Korteweg– de Vries (KdV), Kaup–Boussinesq (KB) and Camassa–Holm (CH). Finite-gap solutions are obtained via a finite-reduction map, defined algebraically, which sends solutions of a Stäckel system to solutions of the BKM PDE.
