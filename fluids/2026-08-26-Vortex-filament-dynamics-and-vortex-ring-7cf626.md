# Vortex filament dynamics and vortex ring motion revisited

- **作者 (Authors)**: Andrew D Gilbert
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-26
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25827v1)

## 中文摘要

理想不可压缩流体中细长涡旋的运动是流体动力学中的一个经典问题。涡环速度的公式可以追溯到十九世纪开尔文、亥姆霍兹、希克斯和戴森的工作，而最近创建了许多模型来模拟一般形状的细长涡管的运动。对于位于曲线 C(t) 附近的涡流管或细丝，测量细长度的相关小参数是管半径除以 C 的曲率半径。本文通过引入与细长涡旋中涡旋表面几何形状密切相关的坐标系，重新审视了这一系列经典问题。该坐标系中流体元的运动具有作用角形式，因此涡量方程从根本上简化。然而，与此同时，涡旋的形状或演化的大多数方面都被纳入坐标的描述中，特别是相应的度量和体积形式。   由于坐标系是非正交且与时间相关的，最容易使用微分几何工具来描述涡量和坐标系的结构，并建立了通​​用的数学框架。所得到的方程组尽可能地仅假设涡旋细长，但允许涡核和曲线 C(t) 的任意运动和扭曲。该模型用于计算具有任意轴流的细长涡环的运动，直接求解扰动涡度表面的形状，并给出与早期研究一致的结果。本文建立的总体框架适合于未来研究中涡旋运动和相互作用的简化方程的发展。

## 英文摘要

The motion of a slender vortex in ideal incompressible fluid is a classic problem in hydrodynamics. Formulae for the velocity of a vortex ring go back to work of Kelvin, Helmholtz, Hicks and Dyson in the nineteenth century, while more recently a number of models have been created for simulating the motion of slender tubes of vorticity of general shape. For a vortex tube or filament localised near to a curve C(t), the relevant small parameter to measure slenderness is the tube radius divided by the radius of curvature of C. The present paper revisits this range of classic problems by introducing a coordinate system closely linked to the geometry of vortex surfaces in a slender vortex. The motion of fluid elements in this coordinate system has an action--angle form, and with this the vorticity equation simplifies radically. At the same time, however, most aspects of the shape or evolution of a vortex are thrown into the description of the coordinates, and in particular the corresponding metric and volume form.   As the coordinate system is non-orthogonal and time-dependent, tools of differential geometry are most easily used to describe the structure of both vorticity and coordinate system, and a general mathematical framework is set out. This resulting system of equations is taken as far as possible with only the assumption of vortex slenderness in place, but allowing arbitrary motions and distortions of the vortex core and of the curve C(t). The modelling is applied to calculate the motion of a slender vortex ring with arbitrary axial flow, solving directly for the shape of perturbed vorticity surfaces and giving results in agreement with earlier studies. The general framework set up in this paper is suitable for the development of simplified equations for vortex motion and interaction in future studies.
