# The $L^3$-based strong Onsager theorem

- **作者 (Authors)**: Vikram Giri, Hyunju Kwon, Matthew Novack
- **来源 (Source)**: Ann. of Math.
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-07-01
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2026.204.1.4)

## 中文摘要

在这项工作中，我们证明了三维欧拉方程基于 $L^3$ 的强 Onsager 猜想。我们的主要定理指出，存在耗散总动能、满足局部能量不等式的弱解，并且属于$C^0_t (W^{\frac{1}{3},-3} \cap L^{\infty-})$。更准确地说，对于每个 $\beta\lt \frac{1}{3}$，我们可以在空间 $C^0_t (B^\beta_{3,\infty} \cap L^{\frac{1}{1-3\beta}})$ 中构造这样的解。

---

## 英文摘要

In this work, we prove the $L^3$-based strong Onsager conjecture for the three-dimensional Euler equations. Our main theorem states that there exist weak solutions which dissipate the total kinetic energy, satisfy the local energy inequality, and belong to $C^0_t (W^{\frac{1}{3},-3} \cap L^{\infty-})$. More precisely, for every $\beta\lt \frac{1}{3}$, we can construct such solutions in the space $C^0_t (B^\beta_{3,\infty} \cap L^{\frac{1}{1-3\beta}})$.
