# Global classical solutions to 3D irrotational compressible Euler equations of Chaplygin gases with weakly decaying initial data

- **作者 (Authors)**: Mu Gao, Huicheng Yin
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-13
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13166v1)

## 中文摘要

我们关注 Chaplygin 气体 3D 可压缩等熵欧拉方程的全局经典解问题 \[ \begin{cases} \partial_tρ+ \mathrm{div}(ρv) = 0,\\ \partial_t(ρv) + \mathrm{div}(ρv \otimes v) + \nabla p = 0,\\ ρ(0,x) = \barρ+ \varepsilonρ_0(x),\ v(0,x) = \varepsilon v_0(x)。 \end{cases} \] 其中$\barρ>0$是常数，$\varepsilon>0$很小，状态方程为$p=p(ρ)=P_0-\frac{D}ρ$，其中$P_0$和$D$是一些正常数。对于 Chaplygin 气体的 3D 可压缩欧拉方程，它是具有完全线性简并特征值的多维非线性对称双曲系统的原型，A. Majda 强加了一个基本猜想：它通常有一个全局经典解 $(ρ, v)$，其中 $(ρ-\barρ, v)\in C([0,\infty), H^s(\Bbb R^3)) \cap C^1([0,\infty), H^{s-1}(\Bbb R^3))$ 当 $(ρ_0, v_0)\in H^s(\Bbb R^3)$ 和 $s>\frac52$ 时，除非 $(ρ, v)$ 本身在有限时间内爆炸。本文假设对于任何固定常数$μ$且$0<μ<1/2$，整数$N\geq 15$、$\mathrm{rot}\,v_0(x) \equiv 0$和\[ \|(ρ_0, v_0)\|_{H^{N}(\mathbb{R}^3)}+\sum_{|a|\leq 13} \|\langle x\rangle^{1+μ} \nabla^a(ρ_0,v_0)\|_{L^2(\mathbb{R}^3)} \leq 1, \] 我们证明经典解 $(ρ, v)$ 全局存在。我们的主要内容包括：建立一系列新的能量界限衰变估计、加权点时空 $L^\infty$-$L^2$ 估计以及 Chaplygin 气体 3D 势流方程的加权 Strichartz 型估计。

## 英文摘要

We are concerned with the global classical solution problem of 3D compressible isentropic Euler equations of Chaplygin gases \[ \begin{cases} \partial_tρ+ \mathrm{div}(ρv) = 0,\\ \partial_t(ρv) + \mathrm{div}(ρv \otimes v) + \nabla p = 0,\\ ρ(0,x) = \barρ+ \varepsilonρ_0(x),\ v(0,x) = \varepsilon v_0(x). \end{cases} \]   where $\barρ>0$ is a constant, $\varepsilon>0$ is small, the state equation is $p=p(ρ)=P_0-\frac{D}ρ$ with $P_0$ and $D$ being some positive constants. For the 3D compressible Euler equations of Chaplygin gases, which are a prototype of multidimensional nonlinear symmetric hyperbolic systems with totally linearly degenerate eigenvalues, there is a basic conjecture imposed by A. Majda: it typically has a global classical solution $(ρ, v)$ with $(ρ-\barρ, v)\in C([0,\infty), H^s(\Bbb R^3)) \cap C^1([0,\infty), H^{s-1}(\Bbb R^3))$ when $(ρ_0, v_0)\in H^s(\Bbb R^3)$ with $s>\frac52$ unless $(ρ, v)$ itself blows up in finite time. In this paper, under the assumptions that for any fixed constant $μ$ with $0<μ<1/2$, integer $N\geq 15$, $\mathrm{rot}\,v_0(x) \equiv 0$ and   \[   \|(ρ_0, v_0)\|_{H^{N}(\mathbb{R}^3)}+\sum_{|a|\leq 13}   \|\langle x\rangle^{1+μ}   \nabla^a(ρ_0,v_0)\|_{L^2(\mathbb{R}^3)} \leq 1,   \] we show that the classical solution $(ρ, v)$ exists globally. Our main ingredients include: establishing a series of new decay estimates of energy bounds, weighted pointwise space-time $L^\infty$-$L^2$ estimates and weighted Strichartz-type estimates for the 3D potential flow equation of Chaplygin gases.
