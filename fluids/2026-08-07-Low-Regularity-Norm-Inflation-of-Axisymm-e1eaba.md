# Low Regularity Norm Inflation of Axisymmetric Hall Magnetohydrodynamics in Sobolev Space

- **作者 (Authors)**: Haoming Zhu
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-07
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06730v1)

## 中文摘要

在本文中，我们研究轴对称霍尔磁流体动力学，其中 $u = u^re_r+u^ze_z$ 和 $B = B^θe_θ$。如果系统存在平滑解，我们证明该解必须在 $H^{σ-1}(\mathbb R^3) \times H^σ(\mathbb R^3)$ 中表现出范数膨胀，其中 $1<σ<7/2$。如果霍尔磁流体动力学的平滑解是唯一的，那么这也意味着霍尔磁流体动力学中的标准膨胀。

---

## 英文摘要

In this paper, we study the axisymmetric Hall magnetohydrodynamics where $u = u^re_r+u^ze_z$ and $B = B^θe_θ$. If there is a smooth solution to the system, we proved that this solution must exhibit norm inflation in $H^{σ-1}(\mathbb R^3) \times H^σ(\mathbb R^3)$ for $1<σ<7/2$. If the smooth solution to Hall magnetohydrodynamics is unique, then this also implies a norm inflation in Hall magnetohydrodynamics.
