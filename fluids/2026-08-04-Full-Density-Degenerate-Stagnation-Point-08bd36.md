# Full-Density Degenerate Stagnation Points for Water Waves with General Vorticity

- **作者 (Authors)**: Lili Du, Chunlei Yang
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-04
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04280v1)

## 中文摘要

在本文中，我们重新审视带有涡量的二维行进重力水波的驻点附近自由表面的奇异渐进性。我们证明，除了严格的两侧线性增长机制之外，不存在全密度简并驻点。   我们的主要工具是修正的 Weiss 型单调性公式和修正的 Almgren 型频率公式。它们共同提供了一种完全避免使用贝塞尔型微分不等式的新方法，贝塞尔型微分不等式是先前文献中用来证明全密度简并驻点不存在的重要工具（Ann. I. H. Poincaré-AN, 29, 861--885, 2012）。   结果，我们获得了任意维度上频率归一化爆炸的统一界限以及二维上的强收敛性。作为一种应用，我们将旋转波的斯托克斯猜想扩展到更广泛的涡度分布。

## 英文摘要

In this paper, we revisit the singular asymptotics of the free surface near stagnation points for two-dimensional traveling gravity water waves with vorticity. We prove the nonexistence of full-density degenerate stagnation points beyond the strict two-sided linear growth regime.   Our main tools are a modified Weiss-type monotonicity formula and a modified Almgren-type frequency formula. Together, they provide a new approach that completely avoids the use of a Bessel-type differential inequality, which is an essential tool used in the previous literature to prove the nonexistence of full-density degenerate stagnation points (Ann. I. H. Poincaré-AN, 29, 861--885, 2012).   As consequences, we obtain uniform bounds for frequency-normalized blow-ups in arbitrary dimension and strong convergence in dimension two. As an application, we extend the Stokes conjecture for rotational waves to a broader class of vorticity distributions.
