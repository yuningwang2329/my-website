# On the large-time behavior of strong solutions to the generalized compressible Navier-Stokes-Korteweg system in 2D and 3D for arbitrarily large initial data

- **作者 (Authors)**: Xiangdi Huang, Weili Meng
- **来源 (Source)**: Arxiv (math.AP)
- **日期 (Date)**: 2026-08-11
- **原文链接 (Link)**: [查看原始论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10546v1)

## 中文摘要

在本文中，我们建立了具有任意大初始数据$(ρ_0,u_0)\in H^3\times H^2$的二维和三维周期性可压缩Navier-Stokes-Korteweg系统的强解的全局存在性和大时间行为。粘度系数满足 BD 关系 $μ(ρ)=νρ^α$ 和 $λ(ρ)=2ν(α-1)ρ^α$，而毛细管系数由 $κ(ρ)=\varepsilon^2α^2ρ^{2α-3}$ 给出。对于$α<1$的情况，我们首先利用密度有效速度系统的双抛物线结构，扩大 Gu-Huang-Meng-Zhou [arXiv:2603.11762 (2026)] 中建立的强解全局存在的允许参数范围。然后，我们开发一种时间离散化策略来建立有效速度的统一可积性估计，从而产生统一的密度上限。此外，我们引入了一种新颖的自举论证来连续改进这些可积性估计，这导致密度的统一正下界。最后，我们推导出全局时间高阶估计并证明大时间行为 \[ \left\|ρ(t)-\frac{1}{|\mathbb{T}^N|}\int_{\mathbb T^N}ρ_0 dx\right\|_{H^3} +\|\nabla u(t)\|_{H^1} \longrightarrow0, \qquad t\to\infty, \]对初始数据施加任何小的假设。   对于关键情况 $α=1$，我们改进了 Huang-Meng-Zhang [arXiv:2602.00455 (2026)] 中建立的允许参数范围，并建立了统一的密度上限。

## 英文摘要

In this paper, we establish the global existence and large-time behavior of strong solutions for the two- and three-dimensional periodic compressible Navier-Stokes-Korteweg system with arbitrarily large initial data $(ρ_0,u_0)\in H^3\times H^2$. The viscosity coefficients satisfy the BD relation $μ(ρ)=νρ^α$ and $λ(ρ)=2ν(α-1)ρ^α$, while the capillarity coefficient is given by $κ(ρ)=\varepsilon^2α^2ρ^{2α-3}$. For the case $α<1$, we first enlarge the admissible parameter range for the global existence of strong solutions established in Gu-Huang-Meng-Zhou [arXiv:2603.11762 (2026)] by exploiting the doubly parabolic structure of the density-effective velocity system. We then develop a time-discretization strategy to establish uniform integrability estimates for the effective velocity, yielding a uniform upper bound for the density. Furthermore, we introduce a novel bootstrap argument to successively improve these integrability estimates, which leads to a uniform positive lower bound for the density. Finally, we derive global-in-time higher-order estimates and prove the large-time behavior   \[   \left\|ρ(t)-\frac{1}{|\mathbb{T}^N|}\int_{\mathbb T^N}ρ_0 dx\right\|_{H^3}   +\|\nabla u(t)\|_{H^1}   \longrightarrow0,   \qquad   t\to\infty,   \]   without imposing any smallness assumption on the initial data.   For the critical case $α=1$, we improve the admissible parameter range established in Huang-Meng-Zhang [arXiv:2602.00455 (2026)] and establish a uniform upper bound for the density.
